How Checkpoint 5 works

After a team's Checkpoint 4 QR is successfully scanned, its final clue is revealed.

The team follows the clue and races toward the common final checkpoint.

The final QR is accepted only after that team has completed its full route.

The server-recorded final scan time determines the team's finishing position.
